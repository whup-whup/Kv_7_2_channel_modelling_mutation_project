# Kv_7_2_channel_modelling_mutation_project
Ongoing: modeling the Kv7.2 (KCNQ2) channel and its R581Q mutation in human and rodent layer 5 pyramidal cell models to study effects on neuronal excitability.


This is an ongoing project studying how the R581Q mutation in the Kv7.2 potassium channel (encoded by KCNQ2) affects the excitability of cortical neurons. Kv7.2 carries the M-current, which helps stabilize resting potential and limit repetitive firing, and KCNQ2 variants are linked to neonatal epilepsies.

The approach has three stages: (1) fit a Hodgkin–Huxley-style model of wild-type Kv7.2 to patch-clamp data (rat KCNQ2 expressed in CHO cells, recorded at 35 °C); (2) build an equivalent model of the R581Q variant; (3) insert both into detailed human and [mouse/rat] layer 5 pyramidal cell models and compare how the mutation changes firing.

Current status: building and validating the wild-type channel model (stage 1).
